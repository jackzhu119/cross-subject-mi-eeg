## Direct sources and short evidence excerpts

1. [University 2026 admissions charter](https://bzkzs.cqmu.edu.cn/info/1012/1848.htm), published 2026-05-29 at 11:25 by 招生考试办公室. Article 2 states 学校全称：重庆医科大学（Chongqing Medical University） and identifies the Jinyun Campus at 重庆市沙坪坝区大学城中路61号. Article 41 gives the same contact address with 邮编：401331. Its footer explicitly distinguishes the Yuanjiagang Campus postcode 400016 from the Jinyun Campus postcode 401331.
2. [University undergraduate admissions homepage](https://bzkzs.cqmu.edu.cn/), exact campus-specific footer excerpt: “缙云校区：重庆市沙坪坝区大学城中路61号 邮政编码：401331”.
3. [Official English university overview](https://english.cqmu.edu.cn/About_us/Overview.htm), exact short excerpts: “Chongqing Medical University (CQMU)” and “Jinyun Campus”.
4. [Official English campus location page](https://english.cqmu.edu.cn/About_us/Campus_Life/Location.htm), heading: “Jinyun Campus”; its HTML map marker uses “Chongqing Medical University (Jinyun Campus）”. No English Jinyun street-address wording was found on this inspected page. The general footer's “1 Yixueyuan Road, Yuzhong District, Chongqing 400016” is the other campus's address and must not be substituted for Jinyun.
