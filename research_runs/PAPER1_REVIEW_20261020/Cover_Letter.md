# Cover Letter

Dear PLOS ONE Editorial Office,

Please consider the Research Article “Source-cohort expansion improves weaker-class coverage in cross-subject motor-imagery EEG decoding” as a substantially extended new submission, subject to your assessment of the prior manuscript history described below.

This study investigates a reproducible source-data design strategy for cross-subject motor-imagery EEG decoding. Expanding the source cohort from nine to 60 participants increased weaker-class recall by 21.17 percentage points (95% CI 17.12–25.21) and balanced accuracy by 5.85 points in 85 external participants; 66 improved on both measures. Matched-duration checkpoints and CSP–LDA comparisons further support the empirical source-expansion result. The work includes 108 neural trajectories, automated numerical checks and full saved predictions.

Related manuscript: my separately submitted PLOS ONE manuscript PONE-D-26-52050, “Spatial-filter refitting improves limited-label recalibration in longitudinal motor-imagery EEG,” examines few-label longitudinal CSP/LDA recalibration using Kumar2024. This manuscript instead studies source-cohort expansion with BNCI2014-001 and Yang training data and Dreyer external evaluation. The questions, datasets, model comparisons, analyses and figures are distinct. A copy of the related manuscript is provided for editorial assessment.

I acknowledged this policy violation and sought guidance about a substantially revised future submission. That earlier simultaneous consideration has ended. The present manuscript is not under consideration at any other journal; I do not present it as an invited revision or presume that editorial permission has been granted.
