# Frozen statistical analysis hierarchy for paper-stage studies

This is a prospective plan for *new* Q12–Q16 summaries as of 2026-09-26, not a retroactive preregistration of Q5–Q14. Keep every previously failed, null, and worsened condition visible. Distinguish completed, provisionally reconstructed, planned and independently validated results in every table.

## Estimands and units

- **Q14 amended external primary:** the mean over eligible PhysioNet people of paired `MU_BETA_SHARED − BROAD_EEGNET` subject-level balanced accuracy. For each subject and method, average the same three frozen seed results first; one subject contributes one paired difference. All 109 remain in the denominator under the R2 sampling-rate amendment unless a predefined raw-file integrity gate fails. The artifact-only observed difference `+0.0057329621` and 95% subject-bootstrap interval `[-0.0009668274, 0.0124155510]` are *provisional*, not a passed external validation. Retain the three 128-Hz people separately as a descriptive sensitivity, never as a post hoc exclusion.
- **Q12 source-only mechanism (exploratory):** matched per-subject contrasts `SOURCE_GROUP_DRO − SOURCE_BALANCED_ERM` and `SOURCE_POOLED_WHITEN − frozen_Q8_EEGNet`; augmentation contrasts (channel dropout, gain, both) versus the same frozen Q8 comparator. Report subject-mean BA and each class recall. Matched update/batch budget is required for the GroupDRO/ERM comparison; a different exposure budget is a confound, not evidence of domain generalization.
- **Q13 heterogeneity (exploratory):** for each held-out BNCI target, model and source count, first average seeds within a source subset and then average the four predetermined subsets. The target, not its subset/seed/session/trial, is the inferential unit. Report source-identity spread within each k, paired k trajectories and the distinct frozen k=8 reference. Fixed-20 restricted-source arms cannot isolate source count from the number of optimizer updates; label that confounding and give per-fit update count. Session-only source models evaluate *both* target sessions; average within target before cross-target inference while showing separate target-session panels.
- **Q15 prospective replication:** apply the frozen Q14 or Q15-E002 contrast only to compatible subjects under the exact contract; one Lee subject contributes one paired effect after combining its two sessions, not two independent observations. Analyze Lee and Cho separately. Their two window/model contracts are not pooled as one identical cross-dataset intervention. Q16 associations are descriptive, not a new decoder estimand.

## Reported statistics

For each method/contrast save complete subject × session × seed predictions and a subject table of trial counts, BA, accuracy, macro-F1, κ, confusion matrix, class recalls, predicted-class dominance, three-seed mean/SD, and missingness/failure reason. For each paired contrast report all subject differences, equal-subject mean, median, sample SD, range, direction counts, leave-one-subject-out influence, and standardized paired effect `d_z = mean(diff) / SD(diff)` with a clear undefined rule if SD=0. Effect sizes and direction remain visible even when CI crosses zero.

Use a fixed-seed **20,000-draw subject bootstrap** for percentile 95% CI of the equal-subject mean contrast; resample paired subjects, retaining all three seeds within each sampled subject. Do not resample 34,426 prediction rows as independent data. Report the exact two-sided binomial sign test on non-tied subject differences as a secondary direction diagnostic. In BNCI n=9, bootstrap and sign-test intervals are fragile, and highly overlapping LOSO training sets weaken simple independent-subject interpretations; emphasize estimation and individual data. Any effect-size CI (e.g. bootstrap `d_z`) must follow the same subject resampling and report instability.

One predeclared Q14 primary contrast is not selected from external target scores. For Q12 families, report unadjusted and Holm-adjusted secondary p-values within `DG` and `augmentation` families separately; no pooled post hoc family across unrelated questions. Q13 source count, identity and session and Q16 physiology are exploratory and primarily descriptive; if p-values are added, state the exact tested family and apply Holm within that family. For the two prospective Q15 cohorts, predeclare the same direction, report each cohort's CI, and Holm-adjust the two cohort-level tests if inferential claims are made. A dataset is not a substitute for dozens of independent datasets: with two or three cohorts, present heterogeneity directly and do not claim population-level universality from a trial-weighted pooled score.

## Tables, graphics and interpretation rules

Publish a fixed-order subject forest plot, every subject's confusion matrix or class-recall table, seed trajectory, learning curves and selected epochs, Q13 source-count trajectories with subset spread, session contrasts, and Q16 physiology panels with all subjects. Use fixed color and axis scales where comparisons require them. Show negative and class-collapse subjects; no best-seed/best-subset figure without the full distribution. Primary/secondary/exploratory/sensitivity labels appear in every table. A significance test cannot rescue a broken split, failed validator, missing raw-file hash, or target adaptation. Scientific conclusions must not claim that zero-phase offline filtering would work causally online, that scalp ERD proves model mechanism, or that binary external validation verifies four-class performance.
