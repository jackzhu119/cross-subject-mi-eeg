# Original manuscript figure captions

**Figure 1. Source cohort expansion improves weaker-class coverage for most evaluated participants.** Each point is one of the 85 external participants in Study 1. The horizontal and vertical coordinates are the changes in balanced accuracy (BA) and weaker-class recall (W), respectively, for S60 minus S9 in the prespecified BROAD comparison using source-only tail-recall checkpoint selection. Metrics were calculated for each participant and fitted seed before the three-seed participant average. Teal points mark the 66 participants who improved on both endpoints; the remaining 19 observations are shown in gray. The shaded quadrant denotes positive changes in both endpoints. The gold diamond is the paired cohort mean: +5.85 percentage points in BA and +21.17 points in W. Overall, 72 of 85 participants improved on W. Source: the unchanged saved participant contrasts in `figure_data/Fig1_primary_participant_changes.csv`. This is a descriptive reconstruction of an existing primary comparison; no new statistical test was performed.

**Figure 2. Objective-specific weaker-class profiles in the separate source-risk experiment.** Study 2 compares the combined participant–class tail and within-trial spectral-consistency objective (TC) with each registered comparator: balanced empirical risk minimization (ERM), GroupDRO, TAIL, and consistency alone. A, nested outer-source evaluation in 60 participants, aggregated with equal cohort and then equal participant weights. B, fixed 40-epoch external evaluation in 85 participants. C, source-selected external evaluation in the same 85 participants. Dots are saved paired participant effect estimates in percentage points; horizontal intervals are the original pointwise 95% paired-participant bootstrap intervals conditional on the fitted models and three-seed participant averages. Values to the right of zero favor TC. All 12 registered W contrasts are displayed, including intervals spanning zero. The external evaluations are exploratory because the cohort has prior project exposure. Exact values and original statistical-family labels are retained in `figure_data/Fig2_objective_profiles.csv`. No new model, test, interval or comparator was introduced for this figure.

## Editorial interpretation notes

- Figure 1 concerns joint improvement in BA and W, not a claim that both hand-class recalls increased in every participant.
- Figure 2 preserves the uncertain TC-versus-ERM primary profile. The positive TC-versus-GroupDRO external effects are comparator-specific and do not establish superiority over ERM.
- Study 1 and Study 2 have different processing and implementation identities. Their effect sizes are shown in separate figures, with no pooling or numerical subtraction across studies.
- These are original plots of the author's saved results. No data, figures or wording from the two external reference papers are used.
