# Official PLOS ONE requirements checked 9 October 2026

The current manuscript files and submission materials follow PLOS ONE public guidance. Authenticated submission screens have not been accessed. No journal assessment, acceptance, fee-assistance decision or archive DOI is implied.

| Item | Applied requirement / status | Official source |
|---|---|---|
| Article / length | Research Article; no general word/figure cap; present findings concisely | https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/submission-guidelines |
| Text format | Editable DOCX; double spacing; single column; page/continuous line numbers; ≤3 heading levels | same |
| Title / abstract | Full title ≤250 characters; short title ≤100; abstract ≤300 words, no citations | same |
| Main figures / tables | Standalone figures; captions and tables immediately after first citation | same |
| Figure format | TIFF/EPS; 300–600 dpi; RGB/grayscale; LZW; <10 MB; 6.68–19.05 cm wide, ≤22.23 cm tall; Arial/Times/Symbol 8–12 pt | https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/figures |
| Supporting files | S-number/type labels; file <20 MB; captions after references | https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/supporting-information |
| References | Vancouver, first-citation order, first six authors then et al., standard journal abbreviations | submission guidelines |
| Funding / conflicts | Separate submission-system statements | submission guidelines |
| Corresponding author | ORCID required; author action pending | submission guidelines |
| CRediT / approval | At least one accurate contribution per author; personal confirmations required | submission guidelines |
| Data | Underlying results accessible; third-party access routes, applicable permissions and terms specified | https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/data-availability |
| AI | Tool names, affected content and validity evaluation disclosed in the article; no AI authors | https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/ethical-publishing-practice |
| Ethics | Original collection ethics/consent distinguished from the secondary-analysis requirement; applicable institutional requirements and Human Participants Checklist must be checked; retrospective research-access dates, identifiability and Methods line numbers require actual records | https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/human-subjects-research |
| Cover | ≤1 page; contribution, relation to literature, article type, prior PLOS interactions, appropriate Academic Editors and opposed reviewers; no APC request | submission guidelines |
| Fees | Research Article fee currently $2,477; payment on acceptance, submission-date rate; PFA requested at initial submission | https://plos.org/fees/ |
| Exclusivity / related work | Author must confirm no concurrent consideration or acceptance elsewhere and disclose related manuscripts; current portal confirmation pending | submission guidelines, related manuscripts |
| Editorial scope | Sound methods, supported conclusions and availability; avoid inflated novelty/SOTA claims | https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/criteria-for-publication |

The initial policy retrieval and source hashes are documented in `evidence/official_policy_sources.json`; current policy URLs remain the authority at submission. Figure formatting may be waived at initial review, but this package aims to meet the technical specifications now. Submission-file checks are separate from the author's final approval and institutional requirements.
