# Cover letter

Dear PLOS ONE Editorial Office,

Please advise on consideration of the substantially revised Research Article, **“Source-cohort expansion improves weaker-class coverage in cross-subject motor-imagery EEG decoding.”**

The study evaluates a practical data-design strategy: expanding the source acquisition cohort while holding the full neural-trajectory update budget fixed. A common 18-channel processing contract, source-only checkpoint selection and equal-duration controls support an external comparison in 85 participants. Relative to the nine-person source pool, the 60-person configuration improved weaker-class recall by 21.17 percentage points (95% CI 17.12–25.21) and balanced accuracy by 5.85 points. Sixty-six participants improved on both endpoints. Improvements were also present at common-duration checkpoints and in a conventional spatial-filtering decoder.

The contribution complements existing domain-generalization algorithms by quantifying how source composition changes participant-level class coverage. The complete experiment comprises 108 neural trajectories and nine conventional fits. Code, full saved predictions, all protocol-specified contrasts and supplementary descriptive analyses are supplied. The external evaluation is identified as exploratory.
