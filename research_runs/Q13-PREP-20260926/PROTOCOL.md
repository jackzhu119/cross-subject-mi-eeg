# Q13 source-composition and selection sensitivity — prepared, not run

This protocol implements the previously planned Q13-E001 and **supersedes, without changing or deleting**, the never-executed Q13-E002/E003 plans in `research_runs/NEXT_STAGES/MATRIX.json`. Their all-eight-source selected epochs would have depended on people excluded from a restricted-source fit. The corrected experiments are Q13-E004 and Q13-E005. None changes Q5–Q14 frozen results or the held-out-target policy. The Q13 work remains post-Q9 exploration on the already examined BNCI cohort; it is not independent confirmation.

## Question and hypotheses

Q13-E001 asks whether broad EEGNet versus shared μ/β EEGNet depends on the **source-only** epoch rule. Q13-E004 asks whether the count and identity of source subjects affect performance on an unseen ninth person. Q13-E005 asks whether training only on source session `0train` versus `1test` alters performance on both unseen target sessions. The hypotheses are two-sided: null, harm, zero-recall classes, and reversals are retained.

## Frozen common contract

BNCI2014_001, nine outer LOSO folds, four labels, 22 EEG channels, 250 Hz, run-local order-four zero-phase Butterworth filtering, trial-start `[2.5,5.5)` seconds, all trials including artifact flags, fixed volts-to-microvolts conversion. Q8_BROAD uses 4–40 Hz EEGNet. Q9_MU_BETA_SHARED uses 8–13 and 13–30 Hz with one shared EEGNet and mean logits. The architectures, optimizer, batch size, deterministic mode and three final seeds match frozen Q8/Q9. No target subject is used for model fit, transform fit, normalization, epoch choice, early stopping, condition choice, or seed choice. Source-only inner validation is *not* repeated in Q13-E004/E005; epoch 20 is fixed before these runs.

The Q13-E001 raw-CE comparator for Q8 is **the already finished Q5-E001**; reuse is legal only after validating matching data identity, architecture, training settings, source-only epoch rule and prediction manifest. Q9 raw CE is independently recomputed from the four frozen Q9 source-only inner curves per outer fold, with earliest minimum tie-break; the original Q9 selected epochs are never overwritten. Q8_FIXED20 and Q9_SHARED_FIXED20 each receive 27 new fits. Their 54 models are the matched k=8 and both-source-session anchors for E004/E005. If reuse checks fail, block the comparison and issue a separately versioned amendment—do not silently add runs to the matrix.

For E004, sort the eight source IDs after removing the outer target. For k=2,4,6 take circular windows of length k starting at positions 0,2,4,6. Every source appears exactly k/2 times among the four subsets at each k. Train both methods at exactly 20 epochs × 3 fixed seeds for every subset, yielding `9 × 3 × 4 × 3 × 2 = 648` new fits. The k=8 reference uses the E001 fixed-20 fits. The *natural minibatches-per-epoch* budget means larger k also entails more training examples and optimizer updates; this estimates an operational source-pool effect, **not the isolated causal effect of subject count**. Report update counts and source trial counts. A separately versioned update-matched sensitivity experiment would be required for a causal training-budget dissection.

For E005, use all eight non-target subjects but exactly one of the two acquisition sessions in each condition. Train both methods for 20 epochs × three seeds, yielding `9 × 2 × 2 × 3 = 108` new fits. Always infer on both target sessions; no target-session subset is used for choosing a condition. `0train` versus `1test` is a source-session contrast with equal trial counts. Comparison to both-source-session fixed-20 anchor is descriptive because the source training set size differs.

## Endpoint, statistics and limits

Primary descriptive endpoint is target-subject balanced accuracy. In E004, average the four source-identity subsets **within each target × k × method × seed**, then average the three seeds; use the nine target subjects as units. Show each subset effect as a sensitivity distribution, never as an independent subject. E005 averages seeds within subject before paired session comparisons. Report per-class recall, dominant predicted class, confusion matrices, session-stratified BA, learning curves and fit duration/update counts. Provide paired subject-level effect size and subject-resampling CI; any p-values are exploratory and corrected within predeclared contrast families by Holm. The nine LOSO training sets overlap, and BNCI targets have already informed the overall research direction; do not claim independent or causal confirmation.

Each fit must produce a hashed completion status, checkpoint, fit manifest, prediction probabilities, confusion and metrics. Resume must verify existing hashes and config/runner/data lock; interrupted fits restart only themselves from their fixed seeds, preserving failures. The independent validator must recompute labels, row identity, probabilities, class decisions, BA, class recall, target/source separation, subset balance, fit counts and source-session policy. Its **full scientific pass** also re-loads raw BNCI EEG and replays all 837 checkpoints against saved target probabilities; the cheaper `--artifact-only` mode explicitly cannot issue a scientific pass. No GPU run or cloud deployment is authorized by this preparation.
