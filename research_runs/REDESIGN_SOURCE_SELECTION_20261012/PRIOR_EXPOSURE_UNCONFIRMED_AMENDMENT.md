## Scientific role of the next experiment

The existing first-use version-1 design is preserved. For an execution on Dreyer2023 while the uncertainty remains, a **separately versioned exposure-unconfirmed exploratory external evaluation** replaces the first-use claim. The data were collected previously, and their previous use within the broader research programme cannot be established. The evaluation is therefore a retrospective research extension, with the current model/configuration/statistical decisions locked before its new outcomes, rather than independent confirmatory validation.

It may answer a useful conditional question: how the specified source-only duration rules and complete pipelines perform on the qualified Dreyer acquisition subset under the current locked experiment. It cannot show that an unseen cohort independently confirmed a research-programme hypothesis. Neither a new seed, new common montage, new source model nor an outcome-blind lock today removes unknown historical exposure.

The principal estimand remains the equally participant-weighted mean balanced-accuracy difference **Broad mean-rank minus Broad mean-loss**, averaging the three prescribed final seeds within each participant. The two architecture × three duration-rule grid, native common19 processing, G0 grouping, source-only schedule selection, seeds, endpoints and negative/null retention remain as specified.
