## Why this advances the research

It can replace a confounded historical comparison with a transparently locked common-montage experiment, quantify participant heterogeneity and reveal adverse/null outcomes. Those conclusions are conditional exploratory evidence, not a new SOTA method or an independently demonstrated universal improvement.

Switching to a second candidate now would require a new metadata-only selection and truthful first-use assessment. The already reviewed candidates have documented task, montage, small-sample or resource limitations. Dataset replacement should not be driven by expected favourable scores, and a different name does not itself establish independence. Continue the feasible audited route without disguising this evidential tradeoff.
