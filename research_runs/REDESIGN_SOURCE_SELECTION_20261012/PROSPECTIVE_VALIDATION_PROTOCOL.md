## Data roles and target choice

Historical BNCI/PhysioNet/Cho/Lee remain observed developmental or retrospective evidence. Metadata-based candidate criteria were applied before our target decoder scores: real left/right hand MI, sufficient distinct participants, official provenance/licence, explicit events and authentic common sensors. NETBCI right-hand/rest belongs to the separate second project and is excluded. High-gamma movement and unavailable/mismatched sensor designs are not interchangeable MI benchmarks.

Dreyer2023 is the selected primary candidate because its task, participant inventory, CC BY4.0 original licence and27-sensor metadata permit a common19-sensor operational design. A/B/C are subdivisions of one87-person origin programme. Use only each participant's two acquisition/calibration runs with sham feedback: nominal80 trials,40/class. Exclude A17/A41 before decoder outcomes because the original provider reports unrecoverable electrode order. Do not automatically remove A04/A09/A29/B78/B79: their source-described sensor artifacts are retained in primary eligibility unless a locked raw-only technical guard fails. Planned eligible n is **at most85**, not an observed completed cohort. Full87 and exclusion-of-all-seven provider-flagged summaries, if technically interpretable, are separately labelled sensitivity; corrupt montage persons cannot be silently restored as verified canonical input.

The public sourcedata EDF route and BIDS derivatives must be distinguished from original GDF. The initial bounded data check does not verify every participant or the complete original archive. Before neural fitting/inference, a provenance-qualified input route, all retained file checksums, native cue/event semantics, channel-name identity, physical header scale, cross-version onset conversion and complete run coverage must be committed. Original device calibration cannot be inferred from numeric export agreement. Resolve or retain the hardware-filter/metadata conflict, rather than assuming a sidecar proves signal processing history. If original-conversion provenance remains inadequate for a confirmatory claim, explicitly downgrade to an independent operational derivative-cohort evaluation or block the confirmation gate. Do not mislabel converted EEG as authenticated original GDF.

The sham visual feedback begins cue+1.25s and overlaps the0.5–2.5s scoring crop. This is an MI-containing instructed-task decoding benchmark, not proof of purely cortical MI information or feedback-free neural generalization. No new crop is selected from target performance to hide the difference. Metadata/provider publications were read, but our newly trained target scores have not been computed. Author prior use and unresolved cross-provider participant identity are separate gates.

## Fixed processing and eligibility

Use the actual ordered BNCI–Dreyer19-sensor intersection in config/core_experiment_v1.json, including FCz and excluding P1/P2/POz. Old21-channel Q15 models cannot be used, truncated or supplied interpolated channels as if they were the same model. Re-extract native source EEG and fit new19-channel models. Bands, SOS coefficients, padding, type/units and all library/runtime versions must be recorded before fitting. No target-dependent scaler, covariance alignment, interpolation, BatchNorm update or amplitude retuning is allowed. Header-derived unit conversion is deterministic metadata, not target outcome fitting.

Pre-model raw guards: all declared files must be readable, hash-matched and mapped to their real provider/run identity; retained channel names must be unique and genuine; event labels must have verified left/right cue semantics; each retained context must fit within its real run without artificial concatenation; included samples must be finite. A required-channel flatline (zero dynamic range across a run) or unresolved sample-rate/channel-order/label identity blocks that run. No observed BA, class dominance, physiological descriptor or online success-rate determines eligibility. Record every trial-level technical exclusion and make the same inventory available to all six conditions. Both classes and at least20 valid trials per class per retained participant are required; any loss is logged before scoring. Duplicate file/content/window identities are reported and de-duplicated by physical acquisition identity without multiplying people or events.

If these raw-only guards materially lower the cohort ceiling or reveal source/target protocol incompatibility, commit a metadata-only amendment before any test predictions and reconsider precision openly; do not replace the cohort because its model outcomes are poor.

## Source selection and neural grid

All nine source participants and both sessions supply2,592binary trials, including source artifact flags. G0 source validation groups are[1,2],[3,4],[5,6],[7,8,9], equally weighted folds. One40-epoch trajectory per architecture/fold with seed20261012 supplies the same curves for mean-loss and mean-rank decisions. Mean-loss selects the earliest minimum of mean fold CE; mean-rank ranks all40 epochs separately within each fold (average ties), averages ranks, and takes the earliest minimum. Final all-source seeds20261013/14/15 are prespecified and not ranked by targets.

The grid is two architectures × fixed20/mean-loss/mean-rank × three final seeds. Configured EEGNet has19channels/320timepoints/twooutputs,F1=8,D=2,F2=16,kernel64,dropout.25; Adam lr.001, no weight decay,batch64,no dropped final minibatch. Shared uses one backbone on concatenated μ/β views with averaged logits before CE, pooled training BatchNorm exposure and eval-mode target inference. The nominal core is8inner trajectories+18final fits=26. Identical epoch choices within an architecture/seed may reuse byte-identical checkpoints with a published alias mapping; no fictitious new fit or missing condition. Full configs, selected curves, input/partition digests, learned-state hashes, updates/timing/peak memory and parameter/FLOP accounting must be independently checked.

Fixed20 is source-informed pragmatic comparison, not optimal or noninferior by assumption. Matching epochs does not equalize FLOPs, view count, filtering or normalization exposure, so H3 remains a complete-pipeline contrast. Five preselected alternative source groupings require40extra inner fits, separately budgeted/disabled. They cannot choose a target-successful grouping.
